What is Replit Agent?

Replit Agent is an AI-powered development environment that lets you build full-stack applications from natural language prompts. Unlike traditional IDEs, Replit Agent can understand your intent, scaffold projects, write code, debug, and even deploy—all from a single conversation.

Whether you're a beginner who wants to learn coding, a founder who needs a quick prototype, or a developer who wants to speed up scaffolding, Replit has something for you.

Key Features

🚀 Instant Deployment

Replit Agent automatically deploys your projects to the cloud. No configuration, no Dockerfile, no DevOps knowledge required. Your app is live in minutes.

💬 Natural Language Coding

Tell Replit what you want in plain English. "Build a todo app with user authentication" and watch it create the frontend, backend, database, and API.
